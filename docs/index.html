Two good ideas that rarely meet

Many Windows users end up with files and shortcuts scattered across the desktop. It is quick and visual, but it gets crowded and hard to keep tidy.

Traditional folders give you real organization. What they do not offer is the free-placement experience of a desktop: putting things exactly where you want them.

Desktab brings these two approaches together: the organization of a folder, with the freedom of a desktop.

What Desktab does

A native Windows application that works directly on the folders you already have.

  • Desktop-like workspaces

    Open any folder as a workspace and see its files and folders as icons on a background, with several workspaces available as tabs.

  • Free icon positioning

    Drag icons wherever they make sense to you. Each workspace remembers its own layout.

  • Internal folder navigation

    Move into subfolders and back without leaving the window, with a path bar that shows where you are.

  • Windows Explorer integration

    Drag and drop files between Explorer and Desktab, use the shared clipboard, and rename, copy or move files. Deleted items go to the Recycle Bin.

  • Personalization

    Light or dark, Modern or Classic, rounded or square corners, and your own backgrounds. Available in five interface languages.

  • Lightweight by design

    A native C# / WPF app on .NET. No account, no ads, no telemetry; it does not start with Windows and has no tray icon or background service.
