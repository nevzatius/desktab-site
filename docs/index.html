Why Desktab

Two good ideas that rarely meet

Many Windows users keep active files and shortcuts on the desktop because spatial arrangement is fast and visual. Over time, a single desktop becomes crowded and hard to keep organized across projects.

Traditional folders provide clean hierarchy and structure, but lock your files into rigid lists and grids—removing the ability to group and place items spatially where you expect to find them.

Spatial freedom inside real Windows folders

Desktab bridges both worlds: open any folder on your PC as a dedicated desktop canvas, arrange icons freely per project, and switch between workspaces in tabs—while your files stay in standard Windows folders.

Capabilities

Everything needed to organize real folders

A native Windows application that works directly on the folders you already have—without proprietary databases or cloud lock-in.

  • Desktop-like workspaces

    Open any folder as a visual workspace and view its files and subfolders as icons on a customizable canvas, with multiple workspaces accessible as tabs.

  • Free icon positioning

    Drag icons wherever they make sense to you. Each workspace independently remembers its exact icon positions across sessions.

  • Internal folder navigation

    Move into subfolders and back without leaving the window, using Back, Forward, Up, and an interactive path bar that shows where you are.

  • Windows Explorer integration

    Drag and drop files between Explorer and Desktab, use the shared Windows clipboard, and rename, copy, or move items. Deleted items go safely to the Recycle Bin.

  • Personalization & 10 languages

    Choose Light or Dark theme, Modern or Classic style, rounded or square window corners, and custom backgrounds—with ten built-in interface languages.

  • Lightweight by design

    A native C# / WPF application on .NET. Does not start automatically with Windows, adds no tray icon or background service, and summons anytime with Ctrl+Shift+Space.

Privacy & Reliability

Your files stay on your PC

Desktab is built to respect your machine and your privacy. Every claim below is verifiable in our documentation and Privacy Policy.

  • No account required

    Install from Microsoft Store and start organizing immediately. Desktab has no sign-up flow, user accounts, or developer servers.

  • No advertisements

    Your desktop workspace is a productivity tool, not an ad surface. Desktab contains zero advertising SDKs or promotional banners.

  • No telemetry

    The app contains no analytics, usage tracking, or crash-reporting code. Your files, file names, folder paths, and layouts are never sent to the developer.

  • Ordinary Windows folders

    Workspaces point directly to normal folders on your drive. Layout coordinates are saved locally in your app data folder, and deleted items go to the Recycle Bin.

Release Notes & Roadmap

What’s new and what’s next

Desktab is actively maintained. Here is what shipped in the latest Microsoft Store release and what is currently in development.

Version 1.6.0

Released November 14, 2026
Available now
  • Ten interface languages. Spanish, Italian, Simplified Chinese, Korean, and Japanese join Turkish, English, German, Russian, and Polish.
  • Keyboard workspace switching. Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab cycle to the next or previous workspace; Ctrl+1…9 jump directly to a tab.

Encrypted Workspaces

Planned for Version 1.7.0 · No release date yet
In development
  • Encrypted folder vaults. Convert a workspace folder into an encrypted vault so files are stored encrypted on disk.
  • Local PIN & recovery code. Unlock with a PIN tied to your PC, with a recovery code generated when the vault is created.
  • Verified safe conversion. Existing files are encrypted and verified first; originals are removed only afterward and only with your confirmation.
  • Built-in preview & export. Preview images and text inside Desktab, or export a file to open in another application.
  • Fair access guarantee. Creating an encrypted workspace is planned as a Pro feature; opening an existing vault will always stay possible.

Encrypted workspaces are currently in development and not yet released. Implementation details may change before release, and no release date has been set.
